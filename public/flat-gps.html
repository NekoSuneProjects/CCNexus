Flat Nexus GPS + Quarry Markers

Four flat corner computers provide positioning, quarry bounds, surface Y=0 and the NE service anchor.

Physical layout

NW ●────────────────────────● NE
   │                        │
   │       QUARRY AREA      │
   │                        │
SW ●────────────────────────● SE

Requirements: put all four computer blocks on the same Minecraft Y level, align the rectangle edges with block rows, and mount a normal Wireless Modem on top of every marker computer. The four machines can be as large a rectangle as wireless range permits.

The turtle must hear all four markers at once for a fresh Flat GPS fix. When it later goes underground/out of range, the Fleet agent continues from its saved position using dead reckoning.
